## Passing the classes in short

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tests | **4 tests**, every **3 weeks**, each worth **max 5 pts** |
| During a test | no books, notes or electronic devices |
| Test date and scope | announced **at least a week in advance**; **trial versions** are provided |
| Activity | points for correctly solving tasks in class, added with a **0.25 multiplier**; limit **1 activity point per person per class** |
| Passing condition | **at least 2 pts from every test** and **at least 12 pts from all tests** in total |
| Missing a test | excused **only by a medical certificate**; unexcused — **0 pts** |
| Individual rules (for those entitled) | must be requested **by the end of October** |

### Retake test

- takes place at the **last class**,
- **all** regular tests can be retaken,
- time: **45 minutes** to retake one test, **90 minutes** for two or more,
- a student who had to take the retake test **cannot get a grade higher than good (4.0)**.

### Exam exemption

Students are nominated to Prof. Piotr Chrząstowski for exemption from the exam if they get:
- **at least 4 pts from every test at the first attempt** and
- **at least 6 activity points**.

The final decision is always the Professor's.

### Example of counting points {own}

:::own
An example prepared by the site author. The document says the 12-point threshold concerns the **tests**, and activity points (×0.25) are **added to the test score** — that is how we count them here. If in doubt, ask your instructor.
:::

Tests: 3.5 + 4 + 2.5 + 3 = **13 pts** (each ≥ 2, sum ≥ 12 → passed). Activity: 6 pts × 0.25 = **1.5 pts**. Total **14.5 pts** → grade **3.5** (0.5 pt — i.e. 2 "activities" — short of 4.0).

Exam exemption? No — tests 2.5 and 3 are below 4 pts.

## How to prepare for a test {own}

:::own
Advice from the site author — not part of the official rules.
:::

1. **Solve the trial version** of the test as soon as it is available — under test conditions (no notes, with a clock).
2. Before the test go through the **cheat notes** of the topics in scope (the "Cheat notes" button).
3. Practise **tracing algorithms by hand** (sorting, BST, AVL, heaps, Dijkstra) — the most common type of task. Write the state after every step, as in the solutions on this site.
4. Collect **activity** points — 1 pt in class is 0.25 pt towards your grade, and 6 activity points are a condition for exam-exemption nomination.
5. Don't miss tests — an unexcused absence gives 0 pts, and a retake lowers the maximum grade to 4.0.
