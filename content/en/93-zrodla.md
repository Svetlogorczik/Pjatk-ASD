Papadimitriou — *Computational Complexity*.

## Classes

Problem sets `asd 00` … `asd 12` and "Zasady zaliczenia ćwiczeń ASD 2025". The tasks on this site have **the same logic** but **different data and wording** — each task says which class exercise it is modelled on. Passing details: [Passing the course](page:course).

## Additional materials {own}

:::own
Recommendations from the site author.

- **VisuAlgo** — animations of sorting, BST/AVL trees, heaps and graph algorithms.
- **Cormen** — the chapters on heaps, trees, graphs and greedy algorithms are very close to the course; good for deepening the proofs.
